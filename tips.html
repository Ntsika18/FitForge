Fitness Tips

Science-backed advice to accelerate results

Recovery
Rest Days Are When You Grow

Muscle fibres repair during rest, not during training. Aim for 1–2 full rest days per week, prioritise 7–9 hours of sleep, and include active recovery (walking, stretching) to flush lactic acid and reduce DOMS.

Nutrition
Protein Timing Matters

Consuming 20–40 g of protein within 90 minutes post-workout maximises muscle protein synthesis. Spread total daily protein (0.8–1 g per lb of bodyweight) across 4–5 meals to maintain a positive nitrogen balance all day.

Progressive Overload
Add 2.5 kg Every Two Weeks

The simplest path to strength gains: add a small amount of weight every two weeks on your main lifts. If you can't add weight, add a rep. Log every session — what gets measured gets improved.

Mobility
10-Minute Morning Mobility Routine

A brief morning routine covering hip flexors, thoracic spine, and ankle mobility dramatically reduces injury risk and improves performance in every compound lift. Consistency over intensity — every day beats once a week.
